SafePath

An RL agent is the support assistant inside SafePath, a private app for survivors of gender-based violence. The agent offers; the survivor decides.

Each step it makes one offer: listen, explain an option, ask what she wants, check her safety, turn on discreet mode, ground her, ask consent, refer her, or hand her to a human counsellor. It never sees her real state, only noisy signals. It has to keep her safe and private and get her to the support she chose.
